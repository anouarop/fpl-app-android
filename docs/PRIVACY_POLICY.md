# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 2026*

This privacy policy applies to the "Fantasy Football Companion" Android app (package
`com.shellanddeploy.fpllive`).

## Summary

The app is a **read-only** viewer for Fantasy Premier League data. It does **not** require an
account, does **not** collect, sell, or share your personal information, and shows **no ads**.

## Data we access

- **Your team ID.** You enter a numeric team ID so the app can look up that team's points, rank,
  squad, and history. The ID is stored only on your device.
- **Public game data.** The app fetches public Football/Premier League fantasy data (players,
  fixtures, standings, live points) from the Fantasy Premier League public API
  (`https://fantasy.premierleague.com/api/`). Your team ID is sent to that service as part of a
  normal request, exactly as if you opened the official website.

## Data stored on your device

- A local **cache** of the game data and your team's data (so the app works offline).
- Your **settings** (team ID, poll interval, theme, notification preference).

This data never leaves your device except for the API requests described above.

## Notifications

Gameweek-deadline reminders are generated **entirely on your device** via Android's WorkManager.
No reminder data is sent to any server.

## Third-party services

- **Fantasy Premier League API** — the public, unauthenticated data source.
- The app uses no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party trackers.

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state** — to fetch game data.
- **Notifications** — only if you enable gameweek reminders.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone
under 13.

## Your choices

You can change or clear your team ID and settings at any time in the app's **Settings**, and clear
the local cache from Settings → Storage.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contact the developer at the email address listed on the
store listing.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new "last updated" date.
